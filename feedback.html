CLOSED MARKET TEST

Five questions — then you’re done.

Please answer as if this were a real product you found online. Honest criticism is more useful than encouragement.

1. How useful and relevant was the free result to your actual business?

We are testing whether the result understood the problem and gave you a practical first step.

2. What was the biggest weakness in the experience?

Choose the single issue that would most reduce your likelihood of using it again.

3. After receiving the free result, what would you most likely do?

This is the most important behaviour question. Choose what you would genuinely do, not what sounds supportive.

4. After seeing the A$39 offer or preview, how does the price compare with the value?

If you did not view the kit, select that option.

5. What one change would most improve the product before launch?

Please be specific: content, design, result quality, missing feature, trust, price, implementation help, or anything else.

This closed-test page does not store your answers on a server. “Email my feedback” opens your email app with the structured answers pre-filled. If that does not work, use “Copy feedback” and reply to the invitation email.
