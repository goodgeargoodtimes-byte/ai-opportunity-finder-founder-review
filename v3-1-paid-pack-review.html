1. What an AI assistant does — 2 min

An AI assistant can help draft, rewrite, summarise, organise and analyse information. It can also make mistakes.

You provide facts → AI assists → you check → then you use it.

2. Which type of AI should you use? — 2 min

  • Current facts, prices or changing information → web/search-enabled AI.
  • Drafting or rewriting → AI chat.
  • A document or spreadsheet → file/document mode.
  • Work already inside Outlook, Gmail, Shopify, etc. → built-in AI where suitable.
  • Something repeated automatically → prove the manual version first; automate later.

3. The simple prompt formula — 3 min

TASK — what you want done
CONTEXT — what the AI needs to know
RULES — what it must not guess/change
OUTPUT — what the answer should look like

Do this now

Already have a suitable example ready? Jump to the prompt →

Copy/paste starter prompt

TASK
Draft a short reply to this customer enquiry.

CONTEXT
We are [BUSINESS TYPE] serving [AREA]. Use only the business facts I provide below.

RULES
Do not invent price, availability, scope, guarantees or dates. If something important is missing, write [CONFIRM].

OUTPUT
Write a clear, friendly reply under 150 words with one obvious next step.

BUSINESS FACTS
[PASTE APPROVED FACTS]

CUSTOMER ENQUIRY
[PASTE ENQUIRY]

Before you use the output

Keep a person in control.
Confirm pricing, availability, scope, complaints, refunds and any unusual commitment before sending.
